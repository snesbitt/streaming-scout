01

what it is

Ranks what to watch next from Susan's real Netflix, Prime Video, and Apple TV+ watch history (every title since 2010), matched against her stated taste (spy, action, British procedurals; no sci-fi, fantasy, rom-coms). BritBox, PBS Masterpiece, MGM+, and Paramount+ are reviewed regularly for what's coming next, badged plainly, though they don't sync watch history the way the first three do. Also tracks premieres months ahead, and what she's watching or seeing right now.

Refreshes itself Monday mornings via a scheduled task, or sooner if Susan asks Claude to resync directly.

02

how it's scored

Every match percentage on this page comes from Claude applying three weighted signals to Susan's real watch history, freshly, each time the dashboard rebuilds. There's no stored formula behind these numbers today; there are just documented rules, followed the same way every time.

Signature genre matchfull weight
Actor affinity bonusproportional
Generic genre matchabout 1/3 weight

A genre on Susan's Signature Genres list (British Procedural, Spy Thriller, Mystery, and similar) counts in full; broad buckets like Comedy or Drama count at roughly a third; shared cast with a loved title adds a bonus on top. Loved categories boost further, hated categories force-cap negative, and anything already watched or dismissed is dropped before ranking starts.

Turning this into a literal, stored equation (the same pattern Travel Intelligence already uses for flight scoring) is on the roadmap: see Phase 09.

03

the stack

Static HTML, plus two small Netlify Functions: dismiss (for the "not interested"/"finished" buttons) and status (for the currently-watching/watched buttons). Deploys via GitHub: pushing to main triggers Netlify's Git-connected continuous deployment automatically. Susan is always the one who pushes, from her own terminal, whether it's a change she made herself or one a Claude session prepared and left ready to go — nothing pushes to main unattended.

This site, today

  • Static HTML, no build step
  • Two Netlify Functions: dismiss and status, both Blobs-backed, bounded
  • GitHub repo, auto-deploy on push
  • Dismiss and watch-status sync instantly across devices
  • GitHub Actions checks every push and PR, plus a weekly live-site health check
  • A weekly GitHub Actions job finds and fixes missing permanent exclusions itself, opening a pull request rather than waiting for a chat confirm
  • The watch log, taste profile and exclusion list are guarded against silent truncation on every push, and the live watching/dismissed records are snapshotted into the repo weekly

Where it's headed

  • Full Netlify Functions backend
  • A permanent "currently watching" entry created automatically too, not just flagged when it's missing

what it won't do

  • No accounts, no passwords: reading it requires nothing.
  • No analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking.
  • No live connection to Susan's accounts: one published snapshot, refreshed by hand.
  • No selling or sharing the data.
